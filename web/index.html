How it works

Compare your playing with the score

INPUT

Load a score and recording

Upload a music score (as MIDI, MusicXML, or MEI), then record yourself playing the piece or upload an existing recording.

ANALYSIS

Inspect pitch and timing

Attune compares the notes you played to the score, with adjustable levels of pitch and timing strictness.

REVIEW

Listen and examine notes

Play back your take, inspect individual notes for any intonation or rhythm mistakes, and review your dynamics, vibrato, and timbre.
